DVIT Group Rules and Expectations

1. Confidentiality (Violation May Result in Automatic Termination)
  * Group discussions and participant identities must remain confidential.
  * No recording, screenshots, or sharing group content.
  * Do not engage other participants if seen in public.
  * No other person may be present in your space during sessions.

2. Participation
  * Actively participate in discussions, exercises, and role-plays.
  * Practice honesty, self-reflection, and openness to feedback.
  * Submit homework to reachbeyondcounselingandcoaching@hotmail.com **at least 24 hours before class**.

3. Respectful Behavior
  * No interrupting, yelling, threats, mocking, intimidation, or aggression.
  * Allow others to speak and use respectful language.
  * Respect differences in backgrounds, beliefs, and experiences.

4. Sobriety
  * No alcohol or drug use during the program or attendance under the influence.

5. Violence and Threats
  * Threats or violence toward anyone will not be tolerated.
  * Safety is a priority. All participants must follow the weapons policy.

6. Electronic Devices and Attention
  * Silence and put away phones unless needed for an approved emergency.
  * No driving, watching TV, working, or multitasking during sessions.
  * Remain focused, with your body visible from the waist up, including your hands.

7. Accountability
  * Take responsibility for your actions and behavior.
  * Victim-blaming, minimizing abuse, manipulation, and excuses will be challenged!
