**Important Reminder: Interpersonal Effectiveness vs. Control**

One of the goals of interpersonal effectiveness is learning how to ask for what we want or need in a healthy and respectful manner. However, asking someone to do something does not mean we are trying to _control_ them.

The difference lies in how we communicate and respond to the other person's decision**.** Healthy interpersonal effectiveness involves expressing our needs clearly, respecting boundaries, and understanding that others have the right to disagree or say no.

We can communicate effectively without always getting the outcome we want. Our goal is to improve how we communicate, not to control how others respond.
