Respectful Relationship in details, Give examples of healthy relationship behaviors; identify new skills learned to achieve them

Communication
Without communication, there's no reason for a relationship. So we must be able to share with each other openly. Skills that are learned are 

Active listening: I must be listening without distractions like playing on a device. I must not be interrupting to get something in. I must be asking open questions to continue the conversation. I reflect back what I've heard and confirm that I understand. I must not be judging, fixing, or arguing.

Patience: I can't be waiting my turn to respond, i must be sincerely listening and letting the conversation flow as it flows.

Empathy: I must make the effort to understand not just what they are saying, but what they need from the conversation: Do they want me to pull the nail out of their head, or do they just want to talk about the headaches?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Boundaries

Boundaries aren't meant to hide behind, they are there to keep us safe from behaving in ways that we do not want to. Violating our boundaries for others will lead to harmful feelings toward ourself and others.

Self Awareness: I need to understand my boundaries before I reach them or when I am approaching them and before it becomes a problem.

Assertiveness: Then enforcing those boundaries often requires saying "no" to someone and that often feels like it's going against the flow of being the helpful people we want to be.

Emotional regulation: Sometimes there is disappointment or frustration from the other party and I need to be able to accept that without feeling that I need to get upset that they are upset that I have boundaries.

Listening and respect: As I have boundaries, I must respect the boundaries of others. I must respect others' boundaries and I should not be upset that they express their boundaries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Vulneratbility
To build a successful relationship, I need to be vulnerable. It goes toward building trust and respectful relationships when the people in a relationship can share and understand deeper reasons for their perspectives. I must be honest about why I feel the way I do. Opening up deeply can take time and care to how it's done, so I must understand when it's safe for both me and others in the relationship. If they aren't open or I am not open and try to force something out, can cause damage to the progress of the relationship.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trust
Trust is a composite of many of these things. To build trust, I must be clear in my communications. I must keep and to respect boundaries. I must be vulnerable. I must be accountable for my actions. I must be honest. I will need to show and accept respect. I will need to be patient to develop trust, but if I am sincere and practice these points, trust will have space to grow.
