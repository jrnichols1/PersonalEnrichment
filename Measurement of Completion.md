# Measurement of Completion

Successful completion will be based on the participants’ compliance and observed behaviors as indicated by the DVIT Core Competencies set forth by Washington State as indicated by completing the objectives to the satisfaction of group members and counselor before completion of the program, these include:

* Abstinence - all mood-altering substances. (reporting any prescribed medications) 
* Detail controlling and violent behaviors without blame, minimization, or denial. 
* Describe patterns and beliefs underlying abusive thinking, behaviors, and choices made. 
* Describe the effect of abusive behavior on others, from their point of view – using examples. 
* Demonstrate how to control behavioral patterns to avoid being violent or abusive. 
* Demonstrate technical knowledge of anger, violence, and behavior control techniques. 
* Describe how this treatment program has changed thinking, attitudes, and behaviors. 
* The victim cannot be blamed for the participant’s abuse. 
* The participant must stop all forms of abuse. 
* The abuser is to be held accountable for their actions. 
* The program’s primary concern is for the safety of victims and to bring peace to interpersonal relationships. 
* Stop any violent or threatening behaviors.
